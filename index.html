Questions

What does it do?

Offset Guides adds up to four guides at an exact distance from your ruler origin (0,0) in one click. That's quicker and more accurate than dragging and placing each guide by hand on every job.

How do I set it up?

Run the installer, restart Illustrator, then open Window › Extensions (Legacy) › Offset Guides. The setup guide in the download walks you through it.

Does it work on Windows?

Yes, there is a Mac and Windows installer with a setup guide.

Where do the guides go?

They're measured from your ruler origin (0,0). Put 0,0 at the corner of your dieline, choose your sides and offset, and click Apply Guides.

Which units does it use?

Whatever your document uses. You can also type a unit after the number, such as 3 mm or 0.125 in.

Will it remember my settings?

Yes. It keeps your sides and offset, so the next document is ready to go.
