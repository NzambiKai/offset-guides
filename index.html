Careful about the details.

From your 0,0.
You move the ruler origin. Top and Left land at minus the offset, Bottom and Right at plus the offset. Not from the selection, the artboard, or the edges of the artwork.
Your document's units.
The field follows File › Document Setup. A typed unit, such as 10 mm or 0.25 in, converts to that physical distance. The panel does not change the document's units.
Your settings, ready for the next document.
The sides and offset are still set in the next file. You move 0,0 in that document, then apply. It does not run through a batch of files.
No duplicates.
It will not add a guide where one already exists, and it does not remove yours.
Remembered next time.
The sides and the physical offset you set are there the next time you open the panel.

Questions

How much time does it save?

This dieline took 1 minute 45 seconds by hand. With Offset Guides: 30 seconds. That's 75 seconds saved. Results vary by task.

Which units does it use?

Whatever the document uses, from File › Document Setup. You can type a unit after the number, such as 10 mm or 0.25 in, and the panel converts that physical distance. It does not change the document's units.

Which versions of Illustrator?

The panel is in use on this Mac, under Window › Extensions (Legacy) › Offset Guides. A Windows installer is included and has not been tested on Windows.

Which way do the offsets go?

You place the ruler origin. Top and Left land at minus the offset from that 0,0, and Bottom and Right at plus the offset. Not from the selection or the artboard.
