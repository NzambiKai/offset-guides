How it works

Three steps. No maths on the artboard.

  1. 1

    Set your 0,0

    Drag the ruler origin to the top-left corner of your dieline box, the way you already do. Offset Guides reads it and never moves or overwrites it.

  2. 2

    Pick sides and offset

    Tick any of the four squares (Top, Left, Bottom, Right) and type a Guide Offset in mm. The default is 10 mm.

  3. 3

    Apply Guides

    Click Apply Guides. Guides are added at your chosen offset from your 0,0 point. Didn't want them? Click Undo to step back through earlier applies.

Features

Small panel, does one job properly

  • Your origin stays yours

    It works from the 0,0 you set and never moves, resets or overwrites your ruler origin.

  • Four-square side toggles

    Turn Top, Left, Bottom and Right on or off with one click each. Add only the guides you need.

  • Exact offsets in mm

    One Guide Offset field, 10 mm by default, measured from your 0,0 point. Top and Left go negative (above and left of 0,0), Bottom and Right go positive (below and right of 0,0).

  • No duplicates, nothing removed

    Apply Guides won't double up guides that are already there, and it doesn't delete any of your existing guides.

  • Multi-step Undo

    Undo steps back through your previous applies, one at a time.

  • Docks like any panel

    Open it from Window › Extensions (Legacy) › Offset Guides and dock it next to your other panels.

FAQ

Questions

Which versions of Illustrator does it work with?

Illustrator 2020 and later. It has been tested on Illustrator 2026 (version 30.x) on Mac. Mac and Windows installers are both included.

Does it change my ruler origin or delete my guides?

No. It reads the 0,0 you set and never moves or overwrites it. Apply Guides adds guides at your chosen offset from your 0,0 point. It won't add a guide where one already exists, and it doesn't remove existing guides.

Which way do the offsets go?

Every guide is measured from your 0,0 point, not from the edges of the box. With 0,0 at the top-left of your dieline box and a 10 mm offset, Top is a horizontal guide 10 mm above 0,0 and Left is a vertical guide 10 mm left of 0,0. Bottom is a horizontal guide 10 mm below 0,0 and Right is a vertical guide 10 mm right of 0,0.
